# Privacy Policy for AsphaltCam

Your privacy is paramount. Because AsphaltCam is designed to be a secure, local-only dash cam, **no personal data is ever collected, transmitted, or shared.**

## 1. Data Storage and Transmission
* **Local Only:** All video recordings, audio tracks, and telemetry data (GPS coordinates and speed) are processed and stored strictly on your local device storage.
* **No Cloud Uploads:** There are no remote servers, no user accounts, and no background analytics tracking. Nothing ever leaves your phone.

## 2. Explanation of Permissions
AsphaltCam requests the following system permissions solely to enable core features on your device:
* **Camera:** To record the road ahead or behind you.
* **Location (GPS):** To calculate your speed and log coordinates into a local subtitle file.
* **Microphone:** To capture cabin audio (fully optional and disabled by default).
* **Storage (Videos):** To save and manage your permanent incident clips.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The application does not use any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics frameworks.

## 4. Legal Compliance
You are entirely responsible for ensuring that your use of AsphaltCam complies with local privacy laws and regulations regarding public recording and surveillance in your jurisdiction.
